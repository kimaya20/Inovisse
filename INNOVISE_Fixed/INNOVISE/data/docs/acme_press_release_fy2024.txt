Acme Corp today reported results for fiscal 2024, describing the year as one of the strongest in the company's recent history.

Revenue grew 18% in fiscal 2024 as customers restocked and new programs ramped. Management said demand was broad-based across the portfolio.

The company highlighted continued momentum in its core industrial markets and pointed to a healthy backlog heading into the next fiscal year.

Operating performance was described as solid, with the team executing well against internal plans. No detailed income-statement figures were provided in this release.

Management said the balance sheet remains a source of flexibility and that the company is well positioned to invest in growth. The release did not quantify debt or equity.

Looking ahead, Acme expects to build on this progress and to stay close to its customers. The company thanked employees for a successful year.

This press release contains forward-looking statements and should be read alongside the company's audited financial statements.

The chief executive emphasized pride in the organization and confidence in the strategy. Further details will be discussed on the earnings call.
