We initiated coverage of Acme Corp following the close of fiscal 2022. The company is a mid-size supplier of industrial components with a concentrated OEM customer base.

Fiscal 2022 results looked consistent with a steady industrial supplier. Revenue and earnings were in line with the scale we expected for a firm of this size.

Management spent much of our meeting on cost control and factory utilization. The tone was cautious but constructive.

Management expects operating margin to remain stable. That outlook is the centerpiece of our near-term thesis and assumes no sharp change in mix or input costs.

We view the balance sheet as adequate for the current plan. Leverage did not appear stretched at the time of our review.

Competition is a watch item, particularly if larger peers become more aggressive on price. We will revisit this point as new bids are awarded.

Our note is based on information available in early 2023 and does not update subsequent periods. Readers should not treat this as a current appraisal of fiscal 2024.

This note is for discussion only. We do not place orders or execute transactions; clients make their own decisions.
